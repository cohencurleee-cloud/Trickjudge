How to play

The athlete always starts facing right. The face, nose, chest stripe and shoes make orientation readable while the body rotates.

Frontflip rotates toward the athlete's facing direction. Backflip rotates away from it. Sideflip rolls around the direction of travel. Spins rotate upright around the vertical axis.

Correct calls add score and combo. Wrong calls cost a life.
